# My Favorite Movie: The Witch Part 1: The Subversion

## About the Movie

**The Witch Part 1: The Subversion** is a South Korean movie released in 2018. It was directed by *Park Hoon-jung*.

The story is about a girl named Ja-yoon who lives with her adoptive family. She joins a TV competition, but things start to change when people from her past appear.

## Main Characters

1. **Koo Ja-yoon** – The main character of the movie.
2. **Nobleman** – A man who knows about Ja-yoon's past.
3. **Dr. Baek** – A scientist connected to Ja-yoon.
4. **Mr. Choi** – One of the people from the organization.

## Why I Like This Movie

I like this movie because it has a good story, exciting fights, and unexpected twists. I also like how Ja-yoon's real identity is slowly revealed throughout the movie.

> You may think you know someone, but there could be more to their story.
